La nostra storia

Named after Nonna Alba.

In 1987 Alba Ferrante crossed an ocean with one copper moka pot and a single rule pinned to her apron: nobody drinks alone. Her grandchildren found a sun-bleached corner of 5th Street, hung her portrait beside the till, and gave the place her name.

Thirty-eight years later the rule still stands. Downtown lawyers share a table with Vinewood dreamers, and the terrazza fills the moment the light turns gold.
